BUYING GUIDE / WIRES

How to tell genuine electrical wire from duplicate

THE SHORT ANSWER

Check four things before you pay: the printing and ISI mark on the wire itself, the packaging, the QR code or hologram on the pack, and a GST bill from an authorised dealer. A price far below the market rate is the first warning sign.

Why is duplicate wire dangerous?

From the outside, a duplicate coil looks like the real one. Inside, it has less copper and thinner insulation.

Less copper means the wire heats up when you run a normal load through it. Thin insulation breaks down sooner under that heat. Inside a wall, that is how a fire starts, sometimes years after the house was wired.

1. Read the printing on the wire

Genuine wire has the brand name, the size, the ISI mark and the standard number printed clearly along its whole length. Smudged, uneven or missing printing is a bad sign.

2. Look at the packaging

Compare the box or wrapper with one you know is genuine. Look for spelling mistakes, poor print quality, a different logo or a missing batch number and date.

3. Verify the code on the pack

Many brands now put a QR code or a hologram on each coil, which you can check with the brand's own app or website. If the pack has one, verify it before you pay.

4. Check the price and the bill

If a coil is offered far below the going rate, ask why. Copper has a market price, and nobody can sell real copper wire for much less than it costs.

Always take a proper GST bill. A bill from an authorised dealer is your proof of where the wire came from, and you will need it for any warranty claim.
