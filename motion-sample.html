From our archives · 2000

Two generations,
one address.

Electric Emporium was founded in 1989 by Manoj Katariya on Main Road, Gujri Chowk — Balaghat's first dedicated electrical shop, built on one belief: every home deserves genuine quality at a fair price. This newspaper advertisement, printed in the year 2000, catches the shop at eleven years old and already a landmark. Same address, same family, same promise.
