Sizes

We keep the sizes a house actually needs: the thinner runs for light and fan points, the middle sizes for power sockets, and the heavier sizes for a geyser, an air conditioner or the main incoming line. Earth wire too.

If you are not sure which size a circuit needs, tell us what it will run and how far it has to travel. Undersized wire is the most common mistake we correct at this counter.

Grades: FR, FRLS-H and halogen-free

FR resists catching fire. FRLS-H does that, and gives off less smoke and less halogen gas if it does burn — which matters because in a house fire smoke reaches people before flames do. Halogen-free wire, sold as HFFR and by Havells as Life Shield, goes a step further, and is meant for buildings where a lot of people have to get out: hospitals, schools, hostels.

House wiring is sealed inside the wall. Changing it later means breaking the wall open, so this is the one part of the electrical work worth deciding carefully.

Beyond house wire

Submersible pump cable, armoured cable for underground and outdoor runs, multicore cable and earthing wire are available on order. Bring the pump rating, the load or the run length and we will work out what the job needs.

How to tell genuine wire from a copy

The brand name, the size, the ISI mark and the standard IS 694 are printed along the insulation itself, repeated down the length of the wire — not just on the box or the sticker. Read the wire. We will unroll a length and show you the printing before you buy.
