From Our Archives — 2000
The Katariya Family

Two Generations,
One Address

Electric Emporium was founded in 1989 by Manoj Katariya on Main Road, Gujri Chowk — Balaghat's first dedicated electrical shop. Built on one belief: every home deserves genuine quality at a fair price.

Brothers Sanjay and Vikesh Katariya joined hands to grow the business, and today Yash Katariya carries the same values forward into the next generation.
