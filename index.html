Our Story

35 Years of Lighting Balaghat

Electric Emporium was founded in 1989 by Manoj Katariya on Main Road, Gujri Chowk — Balaghat's first dedicated electrical shop, built on one belief: every home deserves genuine quality at a fair price.

Brothers Sanjay Katariya and Vikesh Katariya joined, and today the next generation — Yash Katariya — carries the same values forward.

We stock only authentic, ISI-certified products from verified brands. No duplicates, no shortcuts — ever.
