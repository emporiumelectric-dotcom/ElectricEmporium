Our Story

35 Years of Lighting Balaghat

Electric Emporium was founded in 1989 by Manoj Katariya on Main Road, Gujri Chowk — with a simple but powerful belief: that every home and business in Balaghat deserves genuine, quality electrical products at a fair price.

What started as Balaghat's first dedicated electrical shop soon grew as brothers Sanjay Katariya and Vikesh Katariya joined hands. Today, the legacy continues with the next generation — Yash Katariya (Manoj ji's son) — carrying the same values forward.

We have always stood firmly against duplicate and substandard products. In an era where counterfeits are rampant, we stock only authentic, ISI-certified products from verified brands — never compromising on quality to chase a lower price.
